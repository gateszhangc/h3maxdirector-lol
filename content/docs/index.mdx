# H3 Max Director

H3 Max Director creates a continuous realtime video stream that responds to live prompts while preserving characters, settings, and story continuity.

## Start a Director session

1. Describe the recurring characters, setting, tone, and opening situation.
2. Add an optional first frame to anchor the visual world.
3. Select 480p or 768p and choose 16:9, 9:16, or 1:1.
4. Sign in with Google and enter the Director workspace.
5. Watch the stream and send new prompts to guide each next beat.

## Available controls

- Continuous realtime video
- Live natural-language direction
- Optional first-frame input
- Character, setting, and story continuity
- 480p and 768p output in landscape, portrait, or square format
