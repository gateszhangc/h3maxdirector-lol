H3 Max Director turns an opening premise into a continuous video stream. Each live prompt can move the action forward while the model preserves the characters, setting, and story already established.

## Establish the scene

Describe the recurring cast, relationships, environment, visual style, and emotional tone before the session begins.

## Direct the camera

Use plain production language such as “the detective enters,” “cut to a handheld close-up,” or “reveal the clue behind the desk.” Give each prompt one clear next beat.

## Preserve what matters

Repeat essential names, relationships, objects, and unresolved story threads when a new direction depends on them. Use an optional first frame when the initial visual identity matters.

## Branch from the current moment

Watch what appears before choosing the next prompt. The strongest directions respond to the current moment instead of resetting the premise.
